## 2. The throughline

**Om takes messy, unglamorous things and makes them work. Sometimes he makes them beautiful.**

Proof, all from real life:
- Payroll, HRMS, QA, PRDs: mess → system.
- 3,382 kg of beach trash → whale sharks.
- A club in single digits → 30+ people, 700+ footfall.
- An awards inbox → 17 wins.
- Captain at 16, elected head at 20. People hand him the ball.

That line is the spine. Every chapter either proves it or shows the person behind it.

## 3. The signature idea: resolution

The site opens on a dithered portrait of Om that is mostly noise: a few hundred dots.
Each chapter you read adds resolution. By the contact section, the portrait is sharp.

Why it earns its place:
- It *is* the brief: "go through this and you know who I am."
- It doubles as the progress indicator. No separate scroll bar needed.
- It is dither with a job, not dither as decoration.
- Skippers see a blurry Om. Readers get the full picture. Small reward for attention.
